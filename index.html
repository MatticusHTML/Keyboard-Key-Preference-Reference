New to switches? A quick guide to feel and sound

Linear

A smooth press with no tactile bump or added click. You can still hear the key hitting the bottom and returning to the top.

Tactile

A bump you feel as you press. That bump does not need a click sound, and a tactile switch can be quiet or quite clacky.

Clicky

A tactile event plus a deliberate audible click. Usually the most noticeable option to people nearby.

Magnetic

A sensing method, with either linear or tactile feel. A compatible keyboard can adjust where the press registers; software does not change the spring's physical weight.

Silent switches use damping to soften impact noise. Thock means a deeper sound; clack means a sharper sound. Neither tells you the volume. Our sound levels are broad expectations, not recordings or decibel measurements.
